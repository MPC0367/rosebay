# Notice — what is in this repository, and whose it is

Read this before making the repository public.

## This is an unsolicited concept site

Rosebay Home Cooking & Café is a **real, trading restaurant** in Nong Nam Daeng,
Pak Chong. This repository is a design and build exercise made *about* them. It
was not commissioned by them, it is not endorsed by them, and it is not their
official website. Nothing here should be presented as if it were.

The placeholder domain `rosebayhomecooking.example` appears in the canonical and
`og:` tags precisely so this can never be mistaken for the live site.

## The photographs are Rosebay's, not ours

Every image in `img/` was taken by or for the restaurant and published on their
own channels — their Instagram (`@rosebayhomecookingcafe`), their Wongnai
listing, and their Google Business listing. Provenance for each set is recorded
in `doc/RESEARCH.md` and reproducible from the manifests in `_qa/`.

**We hold no rights to any of them.** They are included so the site renders as
designed. That is fine for a private repository and for local work. Publishing
them more widely — a public repository, a deployed site, anything indexed —
redistributes a working business's copyrighted photography at scale, under
someone else's name.

If this repository is ever made public, do one of:

1. **Remove `img/` from the repository** (add `/img/` to `.gitignore`, and purge
   it from history — a plain delete leaves every photograph in earlier commits),
   or
2. **Get the restaurant's permission in writing** and record it in this file.

There is no third option where the photographs stay and nobody asked.

## The written content

Dish names, Thai spellings and prices are transcribed from Rosebay's own printed
menu. Hours, the address and the phone number come from their own Instagram and
Facebook posts. Quoted guest reviews are real and attributed to their source.

Nothing is invented — no founder name, no founding year, no chef biography, no
awards, no suppliers, no testimonials, no star rating. `doc/RESEARCH.md` is the
ledger: every fact, where it came from, and what was deliberately withheld and
why.

## The code

The HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PowerShell in this repository are ours and may be
reused freely. The photography, the menu, the trade name and the marks are the
restaurant's.
